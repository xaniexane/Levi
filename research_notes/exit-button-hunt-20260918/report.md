## The pattern: enclosure

1. Free tier cultivates the ecosystem (developers, mods, users) as free labor.
2. The giant converts the ecosystem into a moat: paywall ($42k/mo X
   Enterprise; $12k/50M requests Reddit) or kill (Parse, 1-year runway).
3. The kill is always announced as focus/generosity: "AI scraping,"
   "high-impact products," "comparable to our costs."
4. No exit: ToS amendments ban substitutes (X); blackout ignored (Reddit);
   deletion theater (Discord).
