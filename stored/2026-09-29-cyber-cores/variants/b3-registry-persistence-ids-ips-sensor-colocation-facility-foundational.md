# Registry Persistence Defense for IDS/IPS Sensors in Colocation Facilities (Foundational Maturity)

## Purpose

Run keys, services, Winlogon helpers, and COM hijacks give attackers reboot-surviving execution with minimal footprint. Baseline the autoruns on gold images; alert on deviations, not on the mechanism's existence. Applies to IDS/IPS Sensors in Colocation Facilities at Foundational maturity.

## When to use

- Writes to Run/RunOnce keys, Winlogon Userinit/Shell values, or service ImagePath entries pointing at unusual binaries.
- Sysmon event 13 (registry value set) on autorun locations by non-installer processes, or new services with suspicious binaries.
- Autoruns deltas: new entries appearing between baseline scans on hosts with no software-change record.
- Lens: Best telemetry: alert logs with full packet captures for high-severity events + sensor health metrics. Customer-owned network taps and logging (the colo provider's logs are out of scope — verify contractually).

## Prerequisites

- Written authorization from the asset owner and incident commander before active containment.
- Access to Badge/physical access logs from the facility correlated with remote-hand ticket times.

## Procedure

1. **Authorize and scope.** Open a ticket, confirm written authorization, and preserve evidence before changing anything.
2. **Detect and collect.** Hunt the signals above in Signature alert logs with triggering packet payloads; anomaly-detection baselines and deviations and PCAP ring buffers for incident windows; sensor placement/coverage maps; run `# Suricata/Snort style triage:` then `jq -c 'select(.alert.severity<=2) | {ts:.timestamp, sig:.alert.signature, src:.src_ip, dst:.dest_ip}' /var/log/suricata/eve.json | tail -20`.
3. **Analyze.** Capture the referenced binary: hash, signature, and sandbox detonation to confirm malicious intent. Then: Check the same autorun locations across the fleet — registry persistence is often deployed identically to many hosts.
4. **Contain and harden.** Scheduled Autoruns/Sysmon baselining with alerting on new persistence entries; restrict registry write rights for standard users. Asset layer: Sensors on SPAN/TAP at every trust boundary; signature sets auto-updated; TLS inspection feeding sensors. Environment layer: Locked cabinets, private cages, and escort policies for remote hands.
5. **Document and improve.** Define one escalation trigger in advance (e.g., confirmed credential theft, more than 3 hosts) that moves the case from analyst to incident commander. Record the timeline, indicators, actions taken, and lessons learned.

## Expected outputs

- Scoped timeline; affected assets, accounts, and data with containment status each.
- Hardening verified effective; detection improvements filed.

## Pitfalls

- Cleaning the registry value while the dropper that restores it still runs on a schedule.
- Overlooking user-hive (HKCU) persistence during a 'system-only' triage.
