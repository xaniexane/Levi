# Commitment + Watch

## Purpose

Convert an intention into a commitment — and put a watch on it so it does
not quietly die. A commitment with no check-in is a wish with better
branding.

## When to use

- You have decided to do something that matters and want it to survive your
  future self.
- After defining done, lock the commitment to reach it.
- Anywhere "I'll try" needs to become "I will, and here is how you will know."

## Steps

1. State the commitment in one sentence with a deadline: "I will ___ by ___."
2. Name the check-in: who or what checks, and on what cadence.
3. Record it somewhere you cannot pretend you did not — write it down, tell
   someone, log it.
4. The check-in happens on schedule whether or not there is progress.
   Silence is data.
5. At the deadline: shipped, renegotiated, or released — each stated explicitly.

## Honesty notes

- Renegotiating openly beats quietly slipping. The watch exists to make
  slipping visible, not to punish it.
- Lock fewer things. A locked commitment you break teaches you to stop
  locking — and then nothing is locked.
- If the commitment no longer serves, release it explicitly. Ghosting your
  own commitments is how the system rots.
