# Creative Ignition

## Purpose

Generate raw creative material fast — quantity first, judgment later. Sparks
are ore, not product: the cure for "I have no ideas" is volume, not waiting.

## When to use

- Starting anything creative: a piece, a project, a direction.
- Stuck on an empty page — generate your way out.
- Before cutting: you need material worth cutting.

## Steps

1. State the creative target in one line: what is this for, and who is it for?
2. Set a timer and generate: ten rough ideas minimum. Ugly is fine. Obvious
   is fine. Capture everything.
3. No judging during generation — no ranking, no "but." Capture only.
4. When the timer ends, pick the one or two with the most energy to develop.
5. Park the rest. They are compost, not waste.

## Honesty notes

- Sparks are raw ore. Do not ship the spark — develop it first.
- The first three ideas are usually the obvious ones. Keep going past them.
- Judgment during generation kills generation. Separate the phases ruthlessly.
