# NOW / NEXT / PARK / KILL

## Purpose

Sort a pile fast. Every item gets exactly one bucket, and the pile gets
smaller — not just tidier. Triage that kills nothing is sorting, not triage.

## When to use

- Inbox, task list, or brain-dump overflow.
- After finding the vital few, to place them and dispose of the rest.
- Whenever "I'll get to it" has become a storage strategy.

## Steps

1. Dump everything into one list. All of it.
2. Assign each item to exactly one bucket:
   - NOW: at most three. This week, non-negotiable.
   - NEXT: queued and ordered, with a rough when.
   - PARK: deliberately shelved — with a revisit date, or it is KILL in disguise.
   - KILL: dead. Deleted, declined, or delegated away for good.
3. Kill at least one thing. If nothing dies, you sorted; triage again.
4. NOW feeds the day's three must-happen items. NEXT feeds planning. PARK's
   revisit date gets honored like any other commitment.

## Honesty notes

- PARK without a revisit date is a graveyard with good lighting. Date it or
  kill it.
- The buckets are decisions, not labels. "PARK" chosen to avoid deciding is
  a decision to carry the weight indefinitely.
- Re-triage on a cadence. Piles regrow; that is what the playbook is for.
