# Minimum Viable Day

## Purpose

Define the day's non-negotiables: exactly three things that must happen.
Everything else is bonus. The constraint is the mechanism — it forces the
ranking that a longer list lets you avoid.

## When to use

- Morning planning, or a standup with yourself.
- Days that threaten to dissolve into reactive work.
- After triage, to decide what NOW actually means today.

## Steps

1. Brain-dump everything the day could hold.
2. Force-rank by consequence: what hurts if it does NOT happen today?
3. Cut to exactly three. Not four. Not "three plus a small one."
4. For each of the three, write the done-state in one checkable line.
5. Work them in order. Anything beyond the three is bonus — never guilt.

## Honesty notes

- Exactly three is not a suggestion. The number forces the trade-off; a
  fourth item smuggled in means the ranking was avoided. Re-cut.
- If all three ship, the day was a win — even if the bonus list is untouched.
- A day with no three defined will be defined by whoever shouts loudest.
