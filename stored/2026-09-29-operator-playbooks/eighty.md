# The Vital Few

## Purpose

Most results come from a small slice of the work. This playbook finds that
slice — and, more importantly, names what stops. The stop-doing list is the
deliverable; without it, nothing changed.

## When to use

- The plate is full and everything feels equally urgent.
- Weekly planning, or whenever effort is spreading thin.
- Before adding anything new — this is the gate new work passes through.

## Steps

1. List everything currently on the plate. All of it, no filtering yet.
2. For each item, estimate its real impact if completed. One line each.
   Be brutal — optimism here is how the vital few stay hidden.
3. Rank by impact. Draw the line where the top slice of items carries the
   bulk of the outcomes.
4. Those above the line are the vital few. Protect them: they get the best
   hours and the deepest blocks.
5. Everything below the line goes on the stop-doing list — written down,
   explicit, dated.

## Honesty notes

- "Do it all, just ordered" is not this playbook. Something stops. Name it.
- The stop-doing list must be written, not felt. Unwritten stops do not hold.
- Re-cut weekly. Vital fews rot into busywork if you never re-rank.
