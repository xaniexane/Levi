# REIM × RIEM — Compost Failure, Carry the Genome

A twin pair from the expander canon: **REIM composts failure, RIEM carries
the compost-genome forward.** Failed history is never deleted and never
worshipped — it is broken down into load-bearing pattern and re-seeded.

## The move

1. **REIM — compost.** Take the failure (a dead branch, a broken build, a
   wrong call) and rot it honestly: what exactly failed, what was load
   bearing, what was luck, what was avoidable. No eulogy, no blame —
   just the breakdown.
2. **RIEM — sequence the genome.** Extract the pattern that survives:
   the constraint the failure taught, the check it earned, the shape to
   avoid next time. Name it small enough to carry.
3. **RIEM — re-seed.** Plant the genome into the next attempt: a gate, a
   test, a habit. The failure lives on as immunity, not as scar tissue.

## Pairing law

- Composting without genome-loading **rots** — reflection that never
  changes what happens next.
- Genome-loading without composting is **sterile** — rules with no roots
  in real failure.
- Composting is honest mixing, never staged: the real failure goes in,
  the real pattern comes out.

## When to reach for it

Postmortems, reverted branches, abandoned designs, killed features. Aim
the pair at the insides — the system, the work — and the compost keeps
the organism growing. Advisory only; nothing here acts, sends, or
spends.
