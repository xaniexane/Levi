# Point Inward — Aim the Pairs at the Insides

The operating instruction of the expander canon: **point the pairs at the
insides and watch them grow.** Things go in, more comes out — the honest
mixing does the rest.

## The move

1. **Pick a matched pair.** Only a pair that passed the circuit breaker —
   real fit, real seam.
2. **Aim it at the insides.** The system itself, the work itself,
   whatever needs growing: the games league, the weakness-hunt outputs,
   the NFT apparatus, the training corpus. Never outward at someone
   else's house first.
3. **Watch.** The pairs create the effect (expansion, emergence) —
   never a single engine, never forced. You run the mix; the growth is
   the organism's.

## Pairing law

- The insides keep growing: every combination births new
  features/engines/capabilities, which combine again.
- Fixed module counts contradict the design. The mixable material is
  everything the organism already holds.
- Generous by default: more comes out than went in, or it was not the
  expander at work.

## When to reach for it

Whenever the work stalls and needs growing rather than fixing. Aim the
pair at the insides and watch. Advisory only; nothing here acts, sends,
or spends.
