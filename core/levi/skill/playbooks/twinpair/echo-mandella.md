# Echo × Mandella — Expand and Phantom-Run

A twin pair from the expander canon: **Echo expands, Mandella phantom-runs
under fog.** Never a single engine — the effect (honest expansion) only
exists when the two are matched.

## The move

1. **Echo — expand.** Take the seed (a feature, a plan, a question) and let
   it widen: more variants, more angles, more directions than you need.
   Echo never judges; it only multiplies the surface.
2. **Mandella — stake under fog.** For each expanded variant, stake a
   small, cheap claim under uncertainty — the fog is real, so no claim
   goes in unhedged.
3. **Mandella — phantom-run.** Run each staked variant as a phantom:
   simulate its consequences without committing real resources. Phantoms
   haunt; they surface what the plan hides.

## Pairing law

- Expansion without the phantom-run is **noise** — more surface, no signal.
- Phantom-running without expansion is **stagnation** — rigorous about a
  room you never leave.
- The veto applies: if the two do not fit the material, the circuit
  breaker refuses the pair rather than forcing it.

## When to reach for it

Anything that needs to grow outward before it grows deep: feature
brainstorms, design spaces, strategy options, threat surfaces. Aim the
pair at the insides — the system, the work — and watch what comes out.
Advisory only; nothing here acts, sends, or spends.
