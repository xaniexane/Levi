# Exercise Safety Monitoring

## Purpose

Exercises are safe by design and by monitoring. This function watches safety
continuously: personnel welfare, system impacts, scope compliance — with the authority
to pause or stop. Marked MODERATE because safety monitoring carries stop authority over
live exercise activity.

## When to use

- Throughout exercise execution.
- Continuously, not periodically.

## Prerequisites

- Safety criteria and stop thresholds from the ROE.
- Monitoring feeds: range health, team status, scope-compliance watch.
- Stop authority vested and communicated.

## Procedure

1. Monitor the safety feeds: system health, participant welfare, ROE-compliance indicators.
2. Watch for scope drift: red activity approaching boundaries, blue actions with production
   impact.
3. Enforce thresholds: pause on warning, stop on breach — no deliberation for safety stops.
4. Log every safety action with timestamp and rationale.
5. Brief the exercise director on safety status at each sync.
6. After any stop, lead the safety review before resuming.

## Expected outputs

- Safety-monitoring log.
- Pause/stop records with rationale.
- Safety-review findings.

## Pitfalls

- Monitoring without stop authority — authority must be real and communicated.
- Delayed stops for deliberation — safety stops are immediate.
- No post-stop review — every stop teaches something.

## References

- Safety-management literature; exercise doctrine.

## Exercise role

Safety monitoring is the exercise's circuit breaker — watching always, empowered to
stop, and never overruled on safety.
