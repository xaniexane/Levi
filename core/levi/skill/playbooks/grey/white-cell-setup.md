# White Cell (Exercise Control) Setup

## Purpose

The white cell directs the exercise: controls the scenario, adjudicates disputes,
monitors safety, and calls the stops. This setup stands up the control function with its
tools, communications, and authorities before execution.

## When to use

- Before any multi-team exercise.
- When the scenario needs dynamic control (injects, adjudication).

## Prerequisites

- Exercise director designated with stop authority.
- Scenario and inject schedule.
- Monitoring feeds (grey-team visibility into red and blue).

## Procedure

1. Stand up the control workspace: scenario timeline, team-status boards, comms to all
   teams.
2. Load the inject schedule and adjudication criteria; seal red-team-sensitive material.
3. Establish the safety-monitoring feeds: range health, ROE-compliance watch, participant
   welfare.
4. Define the pause/stop procedures and rehearse the stop call once.
5. Brief all teams on the white cell's role: who to call, for what, how fast.
6. Hand over control to the exercise director at exercise start.

## Expected outputs

- White-cell workspace and comms plan.
- Sealed inject schedule.
- Pause/stop procedures (rehearsed).

## Pitfalls

- No rehearsed stop call — the first real stop shouldn't be the first practice.
- White cell without independent monitoring — control needs its own feeds.
- Unsealed injects leaking to teams — seal and verify.

## References

- Exercise-control literature; military white-cell doctrine (public).

## Exercise role

The white cell is the exercise's conscience and circuit breaker — stood up with
authority before anything runs.
