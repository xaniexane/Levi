# Exercise Participant Onboarding

## Purpose

Participants need briefing, access, and clear conduct rules before the exercise — not
discovered mid-scenario. This onboarding gets everyone ready, badged, and bound by the
ROE.

## When to use

- Before exercise execution, after planning.
- When new participants join mid-exercise.

## Prerequisites

- Participant roster with roles and clearances.
- ROE signed; NDA/conduct agreements ready.

## Procedure

1. Brief each team on the scenario, objectives, ROE, and conduct expectations.
2. Provision access: accounts, VPN, workspace, comms channels — per role.
3. Collect signed conduct agreements and ROE acknowledgments.
4. Run the communications check: every participant reachable on the exercise channels.
5. Brief the safety procedures: how to report a problem, who can stop the exercise.
6. Record onboarding completion per participant.

## Expected outputs

- Onboarding checklist per participant.
- Signed acknowledgments.
- Access-provisioning record.

## Pitfalls

- Unbriefed participants discovering the ROE mid-exercise — brief everyone, no
  exceptions.
- Access provisioned late — access before the start, not during.
- Missing acknowledgments — collect them before execution.
