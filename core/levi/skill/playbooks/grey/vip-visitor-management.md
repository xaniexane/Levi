# VIP and Visitor Management

## Purpose

Leadership visits build support for the exercise program — if they don't disrupt it.
This management plans VIP presence: what they see, when, with who explaining, and the
boundaries that keep the exercise running.

## When to use

- When leadership or external visitors attend.
- For exercises with stakeholder-visibility goals.

## Prerequisites

- Visitor roster with clearances.
- Exercise timeline with good visit windows.

## Procedure

1. Schedule visits around key moments, not during critical team work.
2. Assign escorts: who guides, who explains, who keeps visitors out of team spaces.
3. Brief visitors: non-interference, what they may photograph/ask, classification
   boundaries.
4. Prepare the visit narrative: what the exercise is proving, what they're seeing.
5. Collect visitor feedback — leadership impressions matter for program support.
6. Log the visit for the after-action record.

## Expected outputs

- Visit schedule with escorts.
- Visitor briefing record.
- Visitor feedback.

## Pitfalls

- Unescorted visitors in team spaces — escort always.
- Visits during the hardest exercise moments — schedule around them.
- No narrative — visitors who don't understand what they're seeing don't advocate for
  the program.

## References

- Protocol and liaison literature.

## Exercise role

VIP visits are the program's advocacy engine — managed well, they convert observers into
supporters.
