# Observer Tasking and Placement

## Purpose

Observers watch, record, and learn — without interfering. This tasking places observers
where the exercise's key moments happen, with clear observation objectives and non-
interference rules.

## When to use

- Before exercise execution.
- When hosting VIP, leadership, or training observers.

## Prerequisites

- Observer roster with clearances and objectives.
- Exercise timeline with key moments identified.

## Procedure

1. Assign observers to teams/locations per their objectives and the coverage plan.
2. Brief the non-interference rules: observe, record, ask later — never direct, hint, or
   interrupt.
3. Provide observation guides: what to watch for, what to record, how to timestamp.
4. Establish the observer net (separate from team and control nets).
5. Schedule observer rotations for long exercises.
6. Collect observer notes at exercise end for the after-action.

## Expected outputs

- Observer placement plan.
- Non-interference briefing record.
- Observer notes package.

## Pitfalls

- Observers directing the exercise — non-interference is the rule, briefed and enforced.
- Observers on the wrong net — separate observer communications.
- Uncollected observer notes — collect them before everyone disperses.

## References

- Exercise-observation literature.

## Exercise role

Observers are the exercise's witnesses — placed deliberately, briefed on silence, and
debriefed before they leave.
