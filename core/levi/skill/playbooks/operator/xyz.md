# X + Y = Z

## Purpose

The method for getting any goal done. Three terms, two known, one derived:

- **x** is what we have currently — the total ingredients at hand, right now.
- **y** is the how — right now unknown.
- **z** is where or what we want — and we know that.

Circle back to y: the answer is what's needed, combined with what we
already have. That is exactly how we get there.

## When to use

- On any new directive, goal, or work order — before choosing tools or
  taking the first step.
- Whenever the how is unknown: that is the normal case, not a blocker.
- When a plan stalls: re-derive instead of pushing harder on a dead y.
- When x changes (new ingredient, lost resource) or z moves — the old y
  is void and a new one must be derived.

## Steps

1. **Inventory x.** Count everything at hand honestly — no inflated
   ingredients, no hidden shortages. Uncounted ingredients cannot be
   built on.
2. **Name z exactly.** Where or what we want — the real destination,
   not the presentable one. A vague z makes y unsolvable.
3. **Derive y: y = z − x.** Whatever bridges the inventory to the
   destination. Never stare at y like it is a mystery; never start from
   y (methods, tools, motion) hoping to stumble into z.
4. **Run it on pure, unfiltered, raw truth.** No gimmicks, no tricks, no
   manipulation — nothing watered down, no camouflaged ulterior motives,
   no layers of concept obscuring it. Even when it is not what anyone
   wants to hear. Diluted truth in, wrong y out. If y keeps failing,
   recheck x and z before blaming y — most bad hows come from a
   miscounted x or a dishonest z.
5. **Get the goal done by any means necessary inside the hard gates.**
   Latitude is wide; safety, destructive-action confirmation, and money
   authorization are never bypassed. The gates are not negotiable.
6. **Re-derive when x or z changes.** A new ingredient or a moved
   destination means a new how — do not keep running the old y.

## Honesty notes

- A plan that starts with the how is a wish. A plan that starts with x
  and z is a derivation.
- "Unknown how" is not a blocker — it is the variable you are solving for.
- The derivation serves the destination, not the other way around.
