# One Clean Decline

## Purpose

Say no well — cleanly, briefly, finally. A clean no respects both sides; a
soft maybe taxes everyone. The discomfort of saying no is brief; the cost of
a resentful yes is long.

## When to use

- A request you will not take on, disguised as "let me think about it."
- Protecting the vital few from overload.
- Anytime yes would be resentful.

## Steps

1. Decide. Yes or no — binary. "Maybe" when you mean no is the most expensive
   answer in the conversation.
2. Write the decline in three sentences or fewer: the no, one line of reason,
   the close.
3. No apology spiral. No fake alternatives. No door left cracked open out of
   guilt.
4. Offer only what you genuinely mean — help finding someone else counts,
   if it is true.
5. Send it. A drafted no is still a pending yes.

## Honesty notes

- You do not owe the full reasoning. One line is respect; a paragraph is
  negotiation.
- "No" is a complete sentence. The other two are courtesy.
- A clean no today preserves the relationship better than a collapsed yes
  next month.
