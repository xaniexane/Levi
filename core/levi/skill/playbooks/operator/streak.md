# Ship / Deep-Work Days

## Purpose

Track consecutive days of shipping or deep work. The streak is a mirror for
consistency — not a chain, and not a verdict on your worth. It measures
whether the practice is alive, nothing more.

## When to use

- Building a daily practice: shipping, writing, deep work, training.
- After a streak breaks — this holds the restart protocol.
- Alongside the daily review, as the long-view metric.

## Steps

1. Define what counts as a streak day: one checkable criterion. Write it down.
2. Record daily: the streak continues or it does not. Binary — no partial-
   credit debates.
3. When the streak breaks: note it in the review, name the cause in one line,
   restart the next day.
4. Milestones are for noticing, not for pressure — 7, 30, 100. Notice, then
   continue.

## Honesty notes

- The only rule after a break is restart. Guilt is not a strategy.
- Never let the streak outrank the work. A streak preserved by lowering the
  bar is a lie with a number on it.
- The streak measures consistency, not worth. Keep it in that box.
