# Run the Day

Composite: eighty × triage × mvd × deep (absorbed 2026-09-29).

## Purpose

One day, one operating stack. Four phases chained: ① find the vital few,
② triage the pile, ③ pick the day's three, ④ protect the deep block. Each
phase feeds the next; skipping a phase is how days dissolve into reactive
work.

## When to use

- Morning planning, or any day threatening to go reactive.
- The plate is full and everything feels equally urgent.
- Before the hardest work of the day begins.

## Steps

1. ① Vital few: list everything on the plate, rank by real impact, draw the
   line where the top slice carries the bulk of outcomes. Everything below
   the line goes on a written, dated stop-doing list.
2. ② Triage: every item gets exactly one bucket — NOW (at most three),
   NEXT (queued, with a when), PARK (dated revisit, or it is KILL in
   disguise), KILL (dead for good). Kill at least one thing.
3. ③ The three: NOW feeds exactly three must-happen items — not four, not
   "three plus a small one." Write each as one checkable done-state line.
   Work them in order; everything beyond is bonus, never guilt.
4. ④ Deep block: give the hardest of the three its protected block — one
   objective as a done-state, a two-minute start ritual, a written
   distraction contract, one exit condition (objective met or timer ends).
5. Schedule the block like an appointment with someone you respect. If it
   gets interrupted, the ritual restarts it — that is what the ritual is for.

## Honesty notes

- The stop-doing list is the deliverable of phase ①. Without it, nothing changed.
- PARK without a revisit date is a graveyard with good lighting. Date it or kill it.
- A deep block without a distraction contract is a calendar entry, not a commitment.
- If all three ship, the day was a win — even if the bonus list is untouched.
