## The nine proposals

1. **LEVI Harvest — agriculture & food production.**
   *Rationale:* growers, ranchers, and co-ops run seasonal, weather-bound
   operations where fail-closed scheduling and yield pattern awareness
   are incomparable surfaces.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

2. **LEVI Hearthstone — construction & trades.**
   *Rationale:* builders and field crews live in checklists, crew
   coordination, and safety receipts — the industrial edition's sibling,
   but the site moves every month.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

3. **LEVI Hearth — hospitality & food service.**
   *Rationale:* restaurants and hotels run on shift rosters, reservation
   triage, and supply stewardship — high-turnover teams that need
   procedure more than anyone.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

4. **LEVI Counter — retail & storefront commerce.**
   *Rationale:* independent shop owners need inventory awareness, customer
   correspondence, and local campaigns at prices no suite serves.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

5. **LEVI Grid — energy & utilities.**
   *Rationale:* grid and water operators need monitoring support, outage
   coordination drafts, and immutable safety receipts — never control
   systems, same law as industrial.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

6. **LEVI Lantern — science & research.**
   *Rationale:* labs and field researchers need experiment-log support,
   literature weaving, and grant workflows with unbroken provenance.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

7. **LEVI Stage — media & entertainment.**
   *Rationale:* studios, publishers, and creators need production
   pipelines and rights-aware content workflows — creative SI in front,
   legal-grade receipting behind.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

8. **LEVI Route — transportation & fleet.**
   *Rationale:* trucking, transit, and delivery operators need logistics
   awareness and maintenance scheduling — the edition assists; humans
   dispatch, same as first responders.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**

9. **LEVI Altar — faith & congregational life.**
   *Rationale:* churches and congregations need member-care workflows,
   outreach support, and service coordination — the nonprofit edition's
   kindred, priced like the mission deserves.
   **PROPOSED — AWAITING KEEPER'S WORD.**
