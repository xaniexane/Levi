# The Lighthouse Stairs

Four hundred steps, each worn into a shallow bowl by forty years of one
man's feet. The ember-light burns at the top — cold, blue, silent — and
it makes no smoke and gives no heat, only light that seems to fall
upward.

Old Fenwick's voice drifts down: "Mind the stairs. The light minds
itself."

Halfway up, the wind finds the arrow slits and plays them like flutes.

What do you do?
