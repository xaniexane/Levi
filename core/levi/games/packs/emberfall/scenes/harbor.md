# The Harbor at Dusk

The water is hammered copper. Fishing boats come in low with the tide,
and the gulls conduct their usual audit of the catch. Mira Salt stands
at the end of the stone pier with her ledger, counting masts the way
other people count heartbeats.

A cloth net hangs drying on a piling. Somewhere below, something
transparent flicks through the shallows and is gone.

What do you do?
