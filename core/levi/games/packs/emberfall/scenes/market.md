# The Night Market

By ember-light the market is a different country. Sable's stall is
unmarked — everyone knows it anyway — and the sacks of glassfish
glitter faintly, like stars that got tired of the sky.

Pip weaves between the stalls selling tide-pool maps of dubious
provenance. The smell is salt, smoke, and somebody's poor decisions
about spices.

What do you do?
