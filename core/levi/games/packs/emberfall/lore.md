# Emberfall Harbor

Emberfall sits where the river gives up and becomes the sea. Its lighthouse
does not burn oil — it burns ember-light, a cold blue fire dredged from the
deep trenches, and it has not gone out in living memory.

Every spring tide brings the glassfish: small, transparent, and worth more
than their weight in salt to the inland cities. Children catch them in
cloth nets. The Ashen Guild buys them by the sack and asks no questions,
which in Emberfall is a kind of currency all its own.

Three truths every wanderer learns:

1. The lighthouse keeper never leaves the tower. Nobody remembers why.
2. The harbormaster's ledgers are public, and that is exactly why nobody
   reads them.
3. If the ember-light ever turns red, run uphill. Nobody knows why this
   is true either, which is why everyone believes it.
