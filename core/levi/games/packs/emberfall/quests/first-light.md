# Quest: First Light

Old Fenwick has sent word down the stairs — the first message in a
decade. The ember-light flickered last night. Just once. He will not
say what it means, only that someone should check the trench charts
in the harbormaster's office.

Mira Salt will show the charts to anyone who pays the berth fee.
Sable will pay double to make sure nobody looks at them.

Choose a side, or choose neither. The light does not care.
