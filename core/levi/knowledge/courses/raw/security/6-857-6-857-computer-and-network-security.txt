2/3/2015
Pre-requisites
We will be firmer about enforcing the pre-requisites for 6.857 this year.
Do not
sign up for this class unless you have completed your 6.042 requirement already. The following
alternate courses are acceptable: 6.045, 6.046, 6.875, or 18.310. The department may have also approved
other substitutions for 6.042 or approved 6.042-equivalent courses taken elsewhere; that is also OK here.
It is not enough to be taking 6.042 (or one of the other courses) concurrently with 6.857.
You should also have completed , or be taking concurrently, 6.033 or
6.858 (or have department approval for having completed an equivalent course elsewhere).
We will, in rare cases, make exceptions if you have obtained the ``equivalent'' background
in some other way.
If you wish to be
considered for an exception, send an email to
6.857-staff@mit.edu with a description of your year, your
reason for requesting an exception, and what equivalent background you
may have had. Describe also how 6.857 fits into your educational
program and career plans.
If you do not have the pre-reqs as described above, and are not approved for an exception,
please remember to drop the course ; we can not do this for you!
