Introduction

Macronutrients, or nutrients needed in large amounts, include carbohydrates, fats, and proteins and are critical for cells, tissues, and organs. These macronutrients power cellular energy, prevent disease, and facilitate optimal body function. Without macronutrients, life would cease.
