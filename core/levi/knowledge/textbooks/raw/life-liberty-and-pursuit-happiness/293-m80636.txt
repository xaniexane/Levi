✏️ Unit 5 Essay Activity

Standards
Topics: 6.4 The “New South”, 6.7 Labor in the Gilded Age, 6.14 Continuity and Change in Period 6
Learning Objectives: Unit 6C Explain how various factors contributed to continuity and change in the “New South” from 1877 to 1898, Unit 6E Explain the socioeconomic continuities and changes associated with the growth of industrial capitalism from 1865 to 1898, Unit 6L Explain the extent to which industrialization brought change from 1865 to 1898
Historical Developments: 6.1, 6.1.I.C, 6.1.II.B.ii, 6.1.II.D, 6.2, 6.3, 6.3.II.C
Historical Thinking Skills: HTS-1 Developments and Processes, HTS-2 Sourcing and Situation, HTS-3 Source Claims and Evidence, HTS-4 Contextualization, HTS-5 Making Connections, HTS-6 Argumentation
Reasoning Process: RP-3 Continuity and Change
Objectives:

Students will be able to construct an AP-Style Long Essay using resources from Unit 5 of Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness to practice constructing a historical argument.

Students will be able to analyze the AP Long Essay Rubric to reflect on their own skills in the context of the AP Exam.
Resources:
Handout A: Long Essay Rubric
Handout B: Long Essay Options
Handout C: Writing Reflection

I. Warm-Up

Students review Handout A: Long Essay Rubric.

Remind students this rubric is used for scoring all Long Essay Questions on the exam and they should craft their response with these criteria in mind.

Address any student questions on rubric categories.

II. Exploration

Have students read Handout B: Long Essay Options for the essay question and directions.

Clarify any questions on what the prompt is asking.

III. Application

Allow students five to ten minutes to begin brainstorming their responses to their selected prompt.

After brainstorming time has passed, announce that students should now begin writing their essay. It is suggested you time students at this point in the year so they become used to writing for a deadline. It is suggested that students use 35 minutes to plan and write their essay.

IV. Conclusion

After the teacher has had the opportunity to evaluate student essays on the basis of the rubric, students should be provided the opportunity to reflect on their writing progress and set intentional goals for growth, using Handout C: Writing Reflection.
