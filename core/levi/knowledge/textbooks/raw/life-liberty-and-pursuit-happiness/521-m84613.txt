✏️ Unit 8 Essay Activity

Standards
Topics: 9.3 The End of the Cold War, 9.6 Challenges of the 21st Century, 9.7 Causation in Period 9
Theme: WOR America in the World
Learning Objectives: Unit 9C Explain the causes and effects of the end of the Cold War and its legacy, Unit 9F Explain the causes and effects of the domestic and international challenges the United States has faced in the 21st century, Unit 9G Explain the relative significance of the effects of change in the period after 1980 on American national identity
Historical Developments: 9.3.I, 9.3.I.A, 9.3.I.B, 9.3.I.C, 9.3.II, 9.3.II.A, 9.3.II.B
Historical Thinking Skills: HTS-1 Developments and Processes, HTS-3 Source Claims and Evidence, HTS-4 Contextualization, HTS-5 Making Connections, HTS-6 Argumentation
Reasoning Process: RP-2 Causation
Objectives:

Students will be able to construct an AP-Style Long Essay using resources from Unit 8 of Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness to practice constructing a historical argument.

Students will be able to analyze the AP Long Essay Rubric to reflect on their own skills in the context of the AP Exam.
Resources:
Handout A: Long Essay Rubric
Handout B: Long Essay Options
Handout C: Writing Reflection

I. Warm-Up

Students review Handout A: Long Essay Rubric.

Remind students that this rubric is used for scoring all Long Essay Questions on the exam and they should craft their response with these criteria in mind.

Address any student questions on rubric categories.

II. Exploration

Have students read Handout B: Long Essay Options for the essay question and directions.

Clarify any questions on what the prompt is asking.

III. Application

Allow students five to ten minutes to begin brainstorming their responses to their selected prompt.

After brainstorming time has passed, announce that students should now begin writing their essay. It is suggested you time students at this point in the year so they become used to writing for a deadline. It is suggested that students use 35 minutes to plan and write their essay.

IV. Conclusion

After the teacher has had the opportunity to evaluate students’ essays based on the rubric, students should be provided the opportunity to reflect on their writing progress and set intentional goals for growth, using Handout C: Writing Reflection.
