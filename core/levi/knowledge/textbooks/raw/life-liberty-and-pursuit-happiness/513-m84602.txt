Introduction

Founded in 1925, the New Yorker is weekly magazine well known for its illustrated covers commenting on current events. The New Yorker features journalism, commentary, cartoons, and literary works and is read across the United States and internationally. After the September 11 terrorist attack on the World Trade Center in Manhattan in 2001, the New Yorker used its cover illustrations to commemorate the attack itself and comment on its effects.
