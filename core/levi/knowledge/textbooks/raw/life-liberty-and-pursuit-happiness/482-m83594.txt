📝 Vietnam War DBQ

In this lesson, developed by and included with the permission of TeachRock, students will examine 10 primary source documents to answer the essential question: In what ways and to what extent did the Vietnam War change American culture, society, and values?
Suggested Sequencing:

Use this Lesson with the Did U.S. Media Provide Fair and Accurate Coverage of the Tet Offensive? Point-Counterpoint following the Kent State Narrative to discuss the increase of anti–Vietnam War sentiments in the United States into the 1970s.
