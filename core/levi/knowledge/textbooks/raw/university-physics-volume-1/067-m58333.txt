Summary

The incremental work dW in rotating a rigid body about a fixed axis is the sum of the torques about the axis times the incremental angle dθ.

The total work done to rotate a rigid body through an angle θ about a fixed axis is the sum of the torques integrated over the angular displacement. If the torque is a constant as a function of θ, then WAB=τ(θB−θA).

The work-energy theorem relates the rotational work done to the change in rotational kinetic energy: WAB=KB−KA where K=12Iω2.

The power delivered to a system that is rotating about a fixed axis is the torque times the angular velocity, P=τω.
